Comment
I would like this body to rule in favor of consumers' rights over devices they have purchased. Locked
phones are problematic for a number of reasons, not the least of which is that it becomes difficult to switch
carriers. People lead very busy and stressful lives these days. Even the thought of engaging in the process
of getting your phone unlocked can be excessively stressful. This effectively makes locked phones a hurdle
that can defeat competition in carriers. 
Locked phones are difficult to resell, not being "top of the line" anymore, their value is already low. If the
reseller will lose any incentive because it costs money and man-hours to unlock the phone, they typically
throw it away. 
Locked phones are frustrating, wasteful in time and materials and a blatant anti-consumer policy that has no
place in a fair market. Please consider these arguments when you deliberate this matter. Thank you for your
time.
