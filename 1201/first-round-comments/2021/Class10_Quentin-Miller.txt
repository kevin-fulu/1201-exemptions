Comment
Please end the ridiculous use of Section 1201 by US Mobile telecom operators to justify locking mobile
phones to the operators network. There is absolutely no reason why telecom operators need to lock my
phone to their network. It makes it harder for an individual to resell a phone they legally own and
discourages reuse of old phones.
