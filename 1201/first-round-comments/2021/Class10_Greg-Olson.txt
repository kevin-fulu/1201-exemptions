Comment
I am advocating for not letting cell phone carriers sell locked phones. There is no benefit to average
consumers, only for the profits of the corporations who run the cell phone carrier industry. Having phones
locked to one carrier has impacted my family by controlling where we do business and discouraging healthy
and capitalistic competition. We have made efforts to switch carriers in attempt to save money and choose
a service better suited for our needs but have been greatly discouraged from doing so because of the costs
associated with unlocking a phone or being forced to take loan and finance on a new product or service,
directly resulting from phones being able to be locked by carriers. Freedom of choice, financial well being,
and free market are all threatened by the simple act of locking phones to carriers.
