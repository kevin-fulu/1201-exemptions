Comment
Mobile phone companies should not be able to sell any locked 
phones. They make the process for unlocking them so unwieldy 
as to actively encourage the process, artificially locking 
people away from their devices that they have purchased and 
own. Also, the right to repair all items should be further 
protected to prevent the shift to unecessary rent-seeking 
practices invading all aspects of day-to-day life.
