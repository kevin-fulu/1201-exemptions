Comment
I would like to express my support for the exemption of DRM-protected video games in public
and professional libraries for the purposes of historical preservation and public enjoyment. Too
many games, especially games made for niche hardware or requiring defunct software, are
unplayable in any form. Many more games are unplayable unless a player is willing to purchase
and maintain decades' worth of software and hardware or engage in petty piracy. If an exemption
were to be granted, thousands of games once thought lost would be able to be playable far past
when publishers stop supporting them. It would also allow the general population to experience
the history of games without massive inconveniences, increasing knowledge and appreciation for
this important facet of media history.
