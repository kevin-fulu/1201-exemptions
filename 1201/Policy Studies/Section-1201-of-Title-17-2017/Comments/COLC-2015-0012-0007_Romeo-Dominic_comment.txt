Title: Initial Comments -  Dominic Romeo
Posted: 2016-03-04T05:00:00Z
Submitter: Romeo-Dominic

The anti-circumvention provisions of the DMCA are broken. We need to fix them. The provisions are overly broad too easily applied in ways that harm our society. People must be allowed to understand and fix the products they buy. Locking the consumer out of a portion of the product is harmful to repair, security, reliability, and continued operations past the vendors support dates.
