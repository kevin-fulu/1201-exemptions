Title: Additional Comments - Flit, Mike
Posted: 2016-10-28T04:00:00Z
Submitter: Flit-Mike

In my opinion, the purchase of a vehicle means that I should be free to do with it what I want. If I want to switch out the radio in my car, I should be able to. In this context, if the vehicle is autonomous, I should be free to upgrade or change out any portion of my vehicle that I choose. So that means if my autonomous vehicle limits speeds at night to a certain speed, I should be able to upgrade my vehicle and override the software to do that. Or maybe I want it to go slower at night, I should be free to modify the software to enable this.<br/><br/>I think autonomous vehicles should also be limited to an assistance package. Drivers should always be expected to maintain FULL control of their vehicles, and autonomous driving should only intervene in the case of imminent safety issues, such as a cruise control system that will slow the car down to avoid rear ending someone, or automatic emergency braking, or systems that keep the car between lanes. Drivers should NEVER be allowed to take their focus off controlling the vehicle, as any mechanical or electronic fault could become a tragedy.<br/><br/>In short, I think autonomous care should only be driver aid, not driver replacement. Any autonomous systems should require the driver to be interacting with the vehicle and be the main source of control, only intervening in cases of emergency.
