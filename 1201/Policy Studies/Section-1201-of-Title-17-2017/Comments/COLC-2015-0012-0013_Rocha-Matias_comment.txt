Comment ID: COLC-2015-0012-0013
Title: Initial Comments -  Matias Rocha
Posted: 2016-03-04T05:00:00Z
Submitter: Rocha-Matias

The DMCA anti-circumvention provisions are a major violation of human rights. They were deliberately created to render previously-granted safeguards (Fair Use, etc.) against misuse of the American copyright system completely useless by explicitly criminalizing behaviour otherwise specifically protected (such as capturing still images from a DVD for use in review material). Trying to minimize their negative effects through the exemptions process is admirable, but misguided.<br/>Only a complete repeal of the anti-circumvention provisions is compatible with a commitment to justice.
