I support strong, practical, and permanent exemptions to Section 1201 of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act.<br/>The monopoly created by companies against consumers fixing and enhancing devices they have purchased violates the rights of owners and goes directly against the free market system where the purchaser has to have their device repaired only by the manufacturer. This also means for things like farm equipment, they can no longer be fixed in the field. DRM also asserts that purchasers can only use devices for consumption via the companies online marketplace (apple store, google play) which they control the products. Please uphold the rights of true ownership for citizens.
