I wish there was some eloquent and dramatic speech I could give to convey the importance of this. Instead, this will probably sound like a collection of points without even bullet formatting.<br/><br/>The anti-circumvention laws, are not only unnecessary, but are prone for abuse and hinder advances in technology. Many people, especially newer generations who are being raised in the technology rich environment of western society-enjoy experimenting with technology. Taking things apart, seeing how they work. It is human nature to be curious. Without that curiosity we would still be chipping flint before a hunt.<br/><br/>These laws punish curiosity. They punish people for learning. They punish people for making improvements. Meanwhile, they do nothing to help in any efforts against piracy. which I presume is what these provisions were meant to handle.<br/><br/>However, the laws are akin to using a two-handed sword when a scalpel was required. These laws are used as weapons from one corporation against another, with a whole lot of people trapped in the middle. <br/><br/>If we as a society have any real ambition about what the future holds. We have to look at these laws very closely. We have to remove impediments to innovation. That is all that this law really accomplishes. It stops people from learning without paying outrageous costs for the privilege of understanding something they rightfully purchased. These laws are contributing to the US falling behind other nations in science and technology.<br/><br/>Please, do the right thing. Remove these unconscionable barriers of advancement.
