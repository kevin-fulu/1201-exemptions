The DMCA should be revoked.  It harms innovation, but only within America or those wishing to sell software within America.  Outside of America it does not hold.  As other countries have a greater share of the worlds GDP this will only hurt the U.S.A.  As other countries will be able to produce better programmers because they are not afraid of reverse engingeering someone else&#39;s code for fear of violating DMCA.  <br/><br/>In Addition DMCA does not do much to really protect people from illegally copying, but rather mainly restricts those who have legally bought things from using them as they would like for their own personal use.  <br/><br/>Take for instance all these things like Itunes.  It is difficult if not impossible to play the songs one buys on itunes on anything but apple products.  Thus if someone wants to buy a different player at some time in the future they will loose all their songs.  Hence Apple uses DMCA to prevent people from having a choice, not really to prevent people from copying.  This is but one example, and in no way am I saying that only Apple does this, rather many major corporations do this.
