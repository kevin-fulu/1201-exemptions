Title: Additional Comments - Maciulski, Victoria

Submitter: Maciulski-Victoria

I am a 67-year-old handicapped woman who depends on technology to get along.  My only income is Social Security, and it is not very much.<br/><br/>Due to problems with my hands, I cannot write with a pen or pencil and I can only type with my thumbs. I am using the dictation feature on my tablet to write this comment. If I did not have a tablet with the dictation feature, it would be extremely difficult for me to comment on this topic. I also would not be able to send and receive emails, or pay my bills online through my bank.<br/><br/>I need to be able to use electronic devices in order to communicate and get things done. When I purchase a device, it is usually a used device or a refurbished device due to costs. It is possible that when I purchase a used device, that it has been &quot;jailbroken.&quot; (Though that is not my goal when purchasing a used device.)<br/><br/>If I had to only get a device repaired at a place designated by the device maker (and therefore charging higher prices) I would be out of luck. My finances would not allow it.   But many device makers would say that getting my device repaired at a place they don&#39;t authorize or recommend would be voiding the warranty or &quot;jailbreaking&quot; it.<br/><br/>Regarding Section 1201, I would like to see congress adopt a permanent exemption for purposes of diagnosis, maintenance, and repair. I would also like to see some wording that allows overriding copyright on devices for non-infringing purposes.<br/><br/>I know that I am not the only person who is in a position like mine - disabled with very limited income. And I know that other people would benefit in the same way that I would if we were allowed to get our devices repaired anywhere for the best price we can find.<br/><br/>Sincerely,<br/><br/>Victoria<br/>
