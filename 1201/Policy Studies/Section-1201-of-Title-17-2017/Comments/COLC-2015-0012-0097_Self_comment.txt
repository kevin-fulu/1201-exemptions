Regarding Copyright and the Right to Repair,<br/><br/>I continue to be amazed that I am not allowed to repair or modify anything I have bought. When I buy something, I consider it mine, to do with as I please, be it normal use as expected by the manufacturer, other uses not anticipated by the manufacturer, resell the item, repair the item or modify the item.<br/> <br/>Not only should I be able to repair or modify my own items, but I should also be able to hire a third party to perform these actions.<br/> <br/>I fail to see how any of these actions detract from the intent of copyright, which is to ensure that the original creators are rewarded for their efforts. Once I have obtained the item through a legitimate process (as established by the copyright holder), then I have fulfilled my obligations to the copyright holder and I should be able to do with it as I please, with no restrictions what so ever (excluding copying the item and selling clones).<br/><br/>Copyright should not infringe upon my rights to use (including repair or modify) any item legally obtained. Currently MY RIGHTS are being abused and I would like to see them restored.
