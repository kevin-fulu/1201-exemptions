Title: Additional Comments - Hawley, Andy
Posted: 2016-10-28T04:00:00Z
Submitter: Hawley-Andy

Copyright, is an extremely important legal protection for content creators. However I do not support the use of DRM to &quot;lock&quot; customers into additional services. DRM can bu used by manufactures to require the use of approved replacement parts or consumables, or to require service by the manufacturer. <br/>I urge you to reject the any statutory provision that criminalizes circumvention of DRM. Circumvention of DRM is not a crime. Piracy in any form, especially when it infringes copyright should continue to be illegal, however circumventing a digital lock does no harm to the content creator and copy right holder. It is the action after the DRM is broken that may constitute a criminal act. <br/>Allow users the right to break DRM on products they own. Security, Research, and user preference are all valid reason to circumvent DRM. As long as the user does not use them to infringe the copyright holder&#39;s rights. <br/>
