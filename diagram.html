DMCA §1201 Anticircumvention Exemptions & Denied Petitions — Cycle-by-Cycle History

Every class that has won an exemption, plus the substantively-significant proposals that were denied, across nine triennial U.S. Copyright Office §1201 rulemakings (2000–2024). Generated 2026-05-22.

Rows are granted exemptions (regular text) and denied petitions (italic, muted). Both are grouped by equipment/work category. Columns are the nine triennial cycles in chronological order. Each cell shows what happened to that proposal in that cycle, color-coded by status. Matching across cycles is by substance, not by class number.

A red triangle in the top-right corner of any cell indicates a partial denial within that cycle — typically a proposed scope expansion that was rejected even though the underlying class was renewed or granted. Click any cell to open that exemption's full cross-cycle history in the side panel; hovering a cell still shows its per-cycle note. On mobile or in print, the Per-exemption details section below the matrix carries every per-cycle note in expandable form.

Authority. Only the Final Rule grants exemptions. Recommendations are advisory; comments are proposals. Denials cited here all come from the Final Rule (or, where the Final Rule is silent, the Register's Recommendation) — every per-cycle JSON in analysis/ carries the supporting [PAGE N] citation.

Per-exemption details

Full per-cycle context for every row in the matrix above. Click any item to expand; use ⌘F / Ctrl-F to search across all expanded entries. Granted exemptions and denied petitions appear in the same category order as the matrix. This section is fully accessible without hover (works on mobile and in print).

Sources. Every granted-exemption cell traces to the codified § 201.40(b)(N) text in the relevant Final Rule, archived in 1201/Final Rules/YYYY-Final-Rule.{pdf,txt}. Every denied-petition cell traces to the corresponding Final Rule preamble (or, where the Final Rule is silent, the Register's Recommendation). Per-cycle granted records (with verbatim quotes and PDF page citations) are in analysis/cycle-YYYY.json; per-cycle denial records in analysis/cycle-YYYY-denials.json; consolidated cross-cycle status in analysis/exemptions-master.json; independent verification in analysis/verification-report.md.

Coverage of denials. The denial rows here cover (1) every substantively recurring denial topic across cycles, (2) every standalone denied topic from cycles 2010-2024 (where the Final Rule clearly enumerates them), and (3) partial-denial indicators (red corner triangle) on otherwise-granted cells. The cycle-YYYY-denials.json files preserve the full record including minor one-off denials not given their own diagram row.

One historical note. In 2010 the Librarian of Congress overrode the Register on the literary-works-accessibility class — the Register recommended denial but the Librarian granted it anyway (the only known instance of such an override in this archive). It is shown as 'renewed' on the literary-works-accessibility row because the codified rule was issued, but readers should know that the Register-Librarian distinction can matter.
